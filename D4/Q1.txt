Strategic Intent :
Become the most reliable, zero-downtime database platform for high-growth SaaS startups within 12 months, measured by uptime percentage and recovery speed rather than product breadth.


1. OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)
Objective 1: Ensure Continuous Availability of Customer Databases
Key Results
Improve service uptime from 99.9% to 99.99%.
Reduce unplanned outages by 75%.
Maintain database availability during infrastructure failures.
Achieve SLA compliance of 99.99% across all production environments.

Objective 2: Enable Rapid and Automated Recovery
Key Results
Reduce Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR) from 30 minutes to under 5 minutes.
Complete automated failover within 60 seconds.
Achieve zero data loss during failover events.
Automate recovery for 90% of known failure scenarios.

Objective 3: Deliver Zero-Downtime Operations
Key Results
Perform 100% of upgrades without service interruption.
Achieve 100% adoption of rolling deployments.
Eliminate scheduled maintenance downtime.
Reduce deployment-related incidents by 80%.

Objective 4: Build Customer Confidence Through Reliability
Key Results
Increase customer satisfaction related to platform stability to 90%.
Improve Reliability NPS by 25 points.
Provide real-time platform health visibility to customers.
Reduce customer-reported incidents by 60%.
