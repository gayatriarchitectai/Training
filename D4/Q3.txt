Objective:

Deliver a simple and dependable appointment-booking experience so patients can secure appointments quickly without unnecessary waiting.

 

Key Results:

KR1: Reduce average call hold time from 12 minutes to under 5 minutes.

KR2: Increase the percentage of appointment bookings completed within 5 minutes from 65% to 90%.
 
KR3: Improve patient satisfaction with the scheduling experience from 4.0 to 4.6 out of 5.
