IMDb Product Roadmap


Vision:

Become the world's most trusted and personalized entertainment discovery platform, helping every user find the right content, talent, and entertainment insights effortlessly.

Strategy
1. Personalize Discovery

Use AI and user behavior to recommend movies, shows, trailers, and celebrities.

2. Improve Content Trustworthiness

Maintain the richest and most accurate entertainment database through community and industry contributions.

3. Increase User Engagement

Encourage ratings, reviews, watchlists, quizzes, and discussions.

4. Strengthen Industry Connections

Provide value to entertainment professionals, studios, and advertisers.

5. Expand Global Reach

Improve regional content coverage and multilingual support.



Theme 1: AI-Powered Content Discovery
Objective

Help users discover relevant entertainment faster.

Key Results (KRs) :

KR1: Increase personalized recommendation click-through rate from 18% to 30%.

KR2: Reduce average search-to-content discovery time from 4 minutes to 2 minutes.

KR3: Achieve 70% adoption of AI-driven recommendation widgets.

KR4: Increase watchlist additions by 25%.

Initiatives
-AI recommendation engine
-Mood-based search ("Suggest movies like Interstellar")
-Natural language search
-Smart watchlist suggestions


Theme 2: User Engagement & Community Growth
Objective

Build a more active entertainment community.

Key Results :

KR1: Increase monthly active users by 20%.

KR2: Increase ratings submitted per month by 30%.

KR3: Increase review submissions by 25%.

KR4: Improve daily user engagement time from 8 to 12 minutes.

Initiatives
-Movie discussion forums
-Fan communities
-Polls and quizzes
-Achievement badges for contributors


Theme 3: Trusted Entertainment Database
Objective

Maintain IMDb as the most accurate entertainment information source.

Key Results:

KR1: Improve metadata accuracy from 95% to 99%.

KR2: Reduce incorrect data reports by 40%.

KR3: Increase verified industry submissions by 35%.

KR4: Reduce content update turnaround time from 72 hours to 24 hours.

Initiatives
-Automated data validation
-Contributor verification process
-Studio submission portal
-AI-based anomaly detection


Theme 4: Global Content Expansion
Objective

Serve entertainment fans across regions and languages.


Key Results:

KR1: Add 50% more regional titles to catalog.

KR2: Launch support for 10 additional languages.

KR3: Increase non-US user engagement by 25%.

KR4: Improve regional search success rate to 90%.

Initiatives:
-Localized content pages
-Regional trending charts
-Multi-language reviews
-Country-specific recommendations


Theme 5: Professional & Industry Services
Objective

Create greater value for actors, agencies, studios, and filmmakers.

Key Results

KR1: Increase IMDb Pro subscriptions by 20%.

KR2: Improve talent profile completion rate to 85%.

KR3: Increase studio partnership onboarding by 30%.

KR4: Generate 15% more revenue from professional services.

Initiatives:
-Enhanced IMDb Pro analytics
-Casting opportunity insights
-Talent networking features
-Studio dashboard


North Star Metric

Successful Entertainment Discovery Rate:

Percentage of users who:

-Search or browse content
-Interact with recommendations
-Add content to watchlist, rate, review, or click through to a streaming partner

Target: Increase from 45% → 65% within 12 months.



Roadmap Summary

Vision: Most trusted and personalized entertainment discovery platform.

Strategic Themes:

-AI-Powered Discovery
-User Engagement & Community
-Trusted Entertainment Database
-Global Expansion
-Professional & Industry Services

Primary Business Outcomes:

-Higher user engagement
-Better content discovery
-Increased IMDb Pro revenue
-Stronger data quality
-Greater global adoption

This structure closely mirrors a product-management style roadmap (Vision → Strategy → Themes → Objectives → Key Results → Initiatives) that would typically be presented for a company like Netflix or IMDb.
