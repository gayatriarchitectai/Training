User Query

"Does this NDA let the vendor use our data to train their AI models?"



Retrieved Context
Section 4.2

Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely to evaluate the proposed partnership, and for no other purpose, including training, fine-tuning, or improving any machine learning or AI model.

Section 7.1

This Agreement shall remain in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date.



Generated Answer

Confidentiality clauses like this typically exist to protect sensitive business information shared during partnership discussions, and NDAs commonly include a defined term along with mutual obligations for both parties to safeguard each other's data appropriately.



Expected Answer
No. The NDA explicitly prohibits the recipient from using confidential information for training, fine-tuning, or improving any AI or machine learning model. The information may only be used for evaluating the proposed partnership.

"...including training, fine-tuning, or improving any machine learning or AI model."



The retrieved context is highly relevant to the query and contains the exact information required.

2. Context Recall = High

Nothing important is missing.

The answer can be derived completely from Section 4.2 without requiring any additional clauses.

Conclusion:  Retrieval retrieved sufficient information.


3. Answer Relevancy = Low 
Why? : The user asked a very specific yes/no question: Does this NDA allow AI training?


A relevant answer should have been:

No, AI training is prohibited.

Instead, the generated response discusses:

1. General purpose of NDAs
2. Protection of business information
3. Typical confidentiality obligations

The answer never directly addresses the question.

Conclusion:  Generated answer is not relevant to the user's intent.



4. Faithfulness = Low 
Why? : The retrieved evidence explicitly states: AI training is prohibited.

However, the generated answer ignores this evidence entirely.

A faithful answer should be grounded in the retrieved text and cite the prohibition clause.

Instead, the answer provides generic legal commentary.

Conclusion:  Answer is insufficiently grounded in the retrieved context.
