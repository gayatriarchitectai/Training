-L1 Support Engineer validates whether the issue is internal or external.
-GenAI Product Owner assesses user and business impact.
-Google API Support Team investigates vendor-side failures.
-AI Governance Lead authorizes overrides, fallback models, user notifications and documents the incident in design records.
