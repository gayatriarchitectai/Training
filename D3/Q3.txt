Design Choice 1: Model Family

Reasoning Model

Explanation:

Case files are lengthy and require multi-step synthesis.
The model must cross-reference dates, names, prior rulings, and legal information across documents.
This is a complex reasoning task, not simple text generation.
A Reasoning Model is better suited for understanding relationships between facts and producing accurate summaries.


Design Choice 2: Where It Runs

Edge / On-Prem

Explanation:

The scenario states that the agency operates on an air-gapped network.
No connection to any public cloud is permitted.
Government and defense data is highly sensitive and must remain within the agency's controlled environment.
Therefore, the solution must be deployed On-Prem/Edge.
